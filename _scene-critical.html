How to use

Lay the phone flat between the two players (recommended): the lower half is White, the upper half is Black, and Black’s digits are rotated 180° so the player sitting opposite reads them upright. After your move, tap your own half with your palm — the time passes to the opponent.
The arbiter taps “Start game”, then “Start” in the middle, and White’s clock begins.
In landscape the screen splits left/right (Black left, White right) — handy for propping the phone up or for the arbiter and spectators.

Byoyomi: after the main time is gone, every move gets a fresh fixed number of seconds (unused time does not accumulate); overstepping loses. A setting such as 10+3×20 means 10 minutes of main time plus 20 seconds per move for 3 periods — you may overthink on two moves and lose on the third. During byoyomi the digits turn purple and the last 10 seconds tick audibly. Common in Weiqi (Go), Shogi and Xiangqi.
