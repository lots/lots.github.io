MultiGo NeoMultiGo Neo 围棋打谱

Load any SGF and walk its variations on a 13×13 board. Red lines mark what White surrounds, blue lines what Black does, and arrows point at the moves worth talking about — the markup I use when teaching beginners. The browser build runs offline; the desktop app adds GTP engines and native dialogs.读入任意 SGF，在 13×13 棋盘上逐手浏览变化。红线标出白棋围住的地方、蓝线标出黑棋的，箭头指向值得讲的那几手——教初学者时我就这样摆。浏览器版可离线使用，桌面版另有 GTP 引擎与原生文件对话框。

About关于

Weiqi is my hobby.
AI is the other one. 围棋是我的爱好，
AI 是另一个。

I'm LotS. I play weiqi for fun, and I teach beginners. Most of these tools exist for those two things: the clock for the table, MultiGo Neo for the moment a student asks why is this move bad? — dim what isn't the point, draw the connection, put an arrow on the move. I build them for my own practice and my students first; if they help yours too, I'm glad. 我是 LotS。下棋是为了开心，也教初学者。这些工具大多为这两件事而生：棋钟放在桌上计时；学生问「这手棋为什么不好」时，我打开 MultiGo Neo——把无关的地方淡化、画出连络、用箭头指住那一手。先为自己和学生用；如果也能帮到你，那就更好了。

Next up: a GTP client — pick an engine already on your machine (KataGo, GNU Go, Leela Zero), press connect, and play. The connection strings come pre-filled; no command-line flags to remember. 接下来是 GTP 客户端：选一个本机已有的引擎（KataGo、GNU Go、Leela Zero），点一下就能开局。连接串已经备好，不用再记命令行参数。
